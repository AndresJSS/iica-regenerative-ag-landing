Introduction to Regenerative Agriculture

Innovations for Profitable and Resilient Farming

Training modules

Click on each module to explore the specific topics covered

🌱

1. Introduction to Regenerative Agriculture

Explore the basic concepts, key outcomes, and the transition process towards regenerative agriculture. Learn about its global and local importance, community participation, and success stories.

🪱

2. Unlocking soil health

Understand the fundamentals of soil health, composition, and the carbon cycle in agricultural systems. Discover techniques to improve organic matter and fertility using organic fertilizers.

🚜

3. Practices to minimize soil disturbance

Learn about conservation tillage as the foundation for regenerative practices. Dive into minimum tillage methods, cover crops, and soil conservation structures.

🐞

4. Biodiversity and agricultural ecosystems

Master integrated crop management with approaches for pest, disease, and weed management (IWM), focusing on integrating biodiversity into farming systems.

🌳

5. Crop diversity and agroforestry

Compare monoculture with diversified systems. Discover principles and strategies for agricultural diversification, including multi-strata agroforestry planning and design.

💧

Extra: Regenerative water management

A systemic approach to water conservation. Explore the soil-water nexus as the foundation of water resilience, featuring success stories like direct-seeded rice.
